# NioCorp Developments Ltd. (NB) — Decision Log

*Analysis date: 2026-08-04*

---

## 1. Current Stance: **Speculative**

NioCorp is a pre-revenue, single-asset, development-stage critical-minerals company whose value is entirely contingent on future events (construction financing close, permitting completion, successful construction and ramp-up of a novel processing facility) that have not yet occurred. This is not a "Monitor" situation in the sense used for mature operating companies with an ongoing, trackable operating story — it is closer to a project-financing option. Any position should be sized accordingly (small, speculative allocation only, not a core holding), consistent with the [[Bear Case]] and the going-concern qualification that has persisted across all three fiscal years reviewed [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Report of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firms].

## 3. Decision Framework

**What would move this from Speculative to Constructive:**
1. EXIM Bank issues a final debt-financing commitment (or the company closes an equivalent alternative debt package) at terms consistent with or better than the two-thirds-debt funding plan [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 7].
2. The updated Feasibility Study is published with NPV/IRR figures that confirm (inflation-adjusted) or improve on the 2022 study's $2.8B pre-tax NPV / 29.2% pre-tax IRR (Note: external source figures, not found in the 10-Ks themselves — see [[Valuation Check]]).
3. The Radioactive Materials License and other discretionary Nebraska permits are granted.
4. Dilution meaningfully slows following a financing close, evidenced by a materially lower rate of share-count growth in a subsequent fiscal year.

**What would move this to Avoid:**
1. EXIM formally declines the application, or the process stalls past FY2027 without resolution.
2. The updated Feasibility Study shows materially worse economics (lower NPV, higher capex) than the stale 2022 figures.
3. The going-concern qualification persists alongside renewed cash-position deterioration (i.e., the FY2026-and-beyond external cash gains prove to have been consumed without a financing close).
4. A denial (not just delay) of the Radioactive Materials License or another discretionary permit.
5. Further material weaknesses in internal controls, or an unexpected auditor change.

## 4. Key Triggers to Watch (near-term)

- Outcome of the EXIM Bank review process (last known status: step two of four as of the FY2025 10-K, filed September 2025)
- Publication of the updated Elk Creek Feasibility Study (engaged April 2025; not yet published as of this report's August 2026 research date)
- FY2026 10-K (fiscal year ended June 30, 2026) — specifically the going-concern language, updated cash-needs disclosure, and any change in reserve/resource classification following the FY2025 drilling campaign
- Resolution (grant or denial) of the Radioactive Materials License
- Any further scandium or titanium offtake agreement announcements

## 5. Key Risks to Monitor

- Continued shareholder dilution at a pace inconsistent with approaching a financing close
- CBMM (or another dominant incumbent) pricing behavior that could compress the economics underlying any updated feasibility study
- Single-project concentration — there is no other material asset to fall back on if Elk Creek's financing or permitting path fails
- Governance/controls quality, given the disclosed material weakness in internal control over financial reporting as of FYE2025
