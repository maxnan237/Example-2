# NioCorp Developments Ltd. (NB) — Business Overview

*Analysis date: 2026-08-04*

## 1. Corporate Structure and History

[NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1]: "NioCorp Developments Ltd. ("NioCorp," "we," "us," "our," or the "Company") was incorporated under the laws of the Province of British Columbia under the Business Corporations Act (British Columbia) on February 27, 1987, under the name "IPC International Prospector Corp."... On March 4, 2013, we changed our name to "NioCorp Developments Ltd.""

[NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1]: "NioCorp is a United States Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC") reporting company, and we are also a Canadian reporting issuer in British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Ontario, and New Brunswick... Our principal executive office is located at 7000 South Yosemite Street, Suite 115, Centennial, Colorado 80112."

NioCorp is therefore a Canadian-incorporated company that files a standard U.S. 10-K (not a 40-F) — an unusual but confirmed combination — with its operating headquarters in Colorado and its sole material asset located in Nebraska.

**Subsidiaries** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1]:
- 0896800 B.C. Ltd. (British Columbia) — 100% owned
- Elk Creek Resources Corp. ("ECRC") (Delaware) — 80.42% owned — "The business of ECRC is the development of the Elk Creek Project"
- NioCorp Technologies Limited (United Kingdom) — 100% owned — research and development of aluminum-scandium alloys

**2023 up-listing transaction:** NioCorp became Nasdaq-listed (NB / NIOBW) via a business combination with GX Acquisition Corp. II (a SPAC), which closed March 17, 2023, alongside Yorkville convertible-debt and equity-facility financings [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1].

**Stage:** Pre-revenue, development-stage issuer. [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1A]: "As a development stage issuer, the Company has not yet commenced its mining operations and accordingly does not generate any revenue."

**FY2025 development milestone:** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1]: "During fiscal year 2025, the Company initiated a drilling program at the Elk Creek Project to support the conversion of a portion of its current indicated resources into measured resources and the subsequent conversion of a portion of its current probable mineral reserves into proven mineral reserves." This drilling campaign also underpins an in-progress update to the 2022 Feasibility Study (see [[Financial Deep Dive]]).

## 2. The Elk Creek Project

**Location:** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]: "The Elk Creek Property is a carbonatite deposit located in Johnson County, southeast Nebraska, USA... approximately 45 miles southeast of Lincoln, Nebraska."

**Product mix:** Niobium (as ferroniobium), scandium (as Sc₂O₃), titanium dioxide (TiO₂), and potentially rare earth oxides as a by-product [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1]. Rare earths currently sit only in the resource base, not in the mineral reserve — see Section 3.

**Land position:** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]: "The Company currently owns approximately 227 acres of land and associated mineral rights, and an additional 80 acres of surface rights and 40 acres of mineral rights... The Company currently holds six OTPs [option-to-purchase agreements]... The current optioned land package covers an area of 1,272 acres." Combined owned + optioned land covers more than 90% of the Elk Creek Project's mineral resources and mineral reserves [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]. The OTPs "grant the Company an exclusive right to explore and evaluate the property for the term thereof" — a contractual, landowner-level exclusivity rather than a government-granted one.

**Permits held:**
- Air Construction Permit from the State of Nebraska, issued June 2, 2020; NDEE advised April 15, 2022 that periodic extensions are no longer required [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]
- Special Use Permit from Johnson County, received December 24, 2019
- Clean Water Act Section 404 permit secured from USACE under the Nationwide Permit Program — [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]: "No other NEPA-level federal permits are now expected to be required."

**Permits still required (discretionary):** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]: "Three permits from the state of Nebraska are discretionary in nature... Solid Waste Permit; Air Operating Permit; and Radioactive Materials License." On the Radioactive Materials License specifically: "the NDHHS may require additional information and more time to approve the Elk Creek Project under a Broad Scope License." The Company has been working with NDHHS on this permit "since 2014" — over a decade of engagement without resolution, a notable permitting-timeline risk.

**Underground Injection Control (UIC) permit status:** Not found in available filings — only generic federal/state SDWA/UIC regulatory background is discussed; no company-specific UIC application or status is disclosed.

**Construction status:** Not yet begun as of the FY2025 10-K. [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]: "The Board must approve a construction program and budget before construction of the Elk Creek Project can begin. This approval, along with the receipt of all required governmental permits and approvals and the completion of project financings, will determine whether and when construction of the Elk Creek Project can begin." Per the 2022 study, construction timeline is estimated at "39 months to mechanical completion after Authorization to Proceed, plus an additional six months of commissioning and ramp-up... for a total of 45 months." (Note: from external source — subsequent reporting indicates mine portal construction physically began in February 2026, after the FY2025 10-K was filed; see [[Earnings/Q3 FY2026 Update]] and [[Valuation Check]] for details not reflected in the audited FY2025 filing.)

[NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 2]: "There were no changes in the Elk Creek Project mineral resource or reserve estimates as of June 30, 2025, as compared to the Elk Creek Project mineral resource and reserve estimates as of June 30, 2024." — the reserve/resource base is unchanged across all three fiscal years covered in this report; the current FY2025 drilling program is intended to upgrade classification (probable→proven, indicated→measured), not to expand the resource footprint.

## 5. Financing and Capital Structure Status

See [[Financial Deep Dive]] for full detail. In brief: NioCorp had **zero debt outstanding** as of June 30, 2025 (all convertible notes retired during FY2025), cash of $25.6 million, and disclosed a going-concern qualification driven by the gap between available liquidity and the ~$1,141 million initial capex requirement. The company is pursuing up to $800 million in EXIM Bank debt financing (application submitted June 2023, still in process as of the FY2025 filing) and has a DoD-affiliated reimbursement agreement (up to $10.0 million, milestone-based, signed August 4, 2025) to help fund feasibility-study-level engineering and reserve-drilling work [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1].

Funding plan: [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 7]: "Management currently anticipates that it will fund the upfront capital expenditure amount for the Elk Creek Project through a combination of debt and equity financing, with approximately two-thirds of such amount being funded from the net proceeds of debt financing, including the amount of debt that would be represented by the EXIM Financing, if any."

## 7. Competitive Landscape

**Niobium:** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1A]: "The world's largest supplier of niobium, Companhia Brasileira de Metalurgia e Mineração, supplies approximately 85% of the world's niobium. Any attempt to suppress the price of niobium by such supplier... would have negative consequences on the price of niobium and, potentially, on our value." — CBMM (Brazil) is the dominant global producer and the single named competitor, and the filing frames CBMM's pricing power as a direct risk to NioCorp's own value proposition, not merely as market color.

**Scandium:** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1A]: "Supply of scandium has been sporadic in recent years, and there are no primary scandium mines in the world at present. Production primarily occurs as a by-product from existing metallurgical plants, primarily in Russia, Canada, the Philippines, and China. Our management believes the Elk Creek Project would significantly increase the world's supply of scandium trioxide." No specific named scandium competitor companies.

**Titanium:** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1A]: "The Elk Creek Project would produce a small quantity of titanium dioxide relative to other producers." No named TiO₂ competitors — not found in available filings.

**General:** [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1]: "We compete with others in efforts to obtain project financing and resources to advance the Elk Creek Project to construction and commercial operation... These other companies may be better capitalized than us." Competition for NioCorp at this stage is arguably more about competing for capital and engineering/construction resources than for end-market customers. An explicit company self-description as a "North American" or "non-China" critical-minerals supplier was not found in the filings — this positioning is implied by the CBMM/Russia/China sourcing discussion but is not a phrase the company itself uses in the 10-Ks reviewed.

## 11. Summary

NioCorp is a pre-revenue, single-asset development-stage company whose entire investment case rests on successfully financing and constructing the Elk Creek niobium-scandium-titanium project in Nebraska. The company has secured the core operating permits (air, county special use, Clean Water Act) but still needs three discretionary Nebraska state permits, has a reserve base that is 100% Probable (zero Proven), and — critically — has not yet closed the ~$1,141 million of construction capital its own 2022 feasibility study says the project requires, though it is far more liquid than at any prior point in its history following a wave of FY2025-FY2026 equity raises (see [[Financial Deep Dive]] and [[Valuation Check]]). Three confirmed niobium offtake agreements (CMC Cometals, Traxys, ThyssenKrupp) provide revenue-side validation, but scandium and titanium products remain uncontracted, and the company's own filings do not report the NPV/IRR figures that would typically anchor a project-stage investment thesis.
