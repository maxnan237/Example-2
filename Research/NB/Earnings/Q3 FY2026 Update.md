## 2. Cash Flow (nine months ended March 31, 2026)

- Net cash used in operating activities (9-month): $11.9 million (~$1.3M/month average) (Note: from external source, same). This is broadly consistent with the FY2025 full-year operating cash burn of $10.7 million [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows] — the underlying pre-construction operating burn rate does not appear to have materially accelerated yet, despite the much larger cash balance.

## 3. What Drove the Cash Increase

The $393.6 million increase in cash from June 30, 2025 to March 31, 2026 was financed by a sequence of equity offerings occurring after the FY2025 10-K was filed (Note: from external source, SEC filing-metadata summaries and niocorp.com press releases, 2025-2026):

| Offering | Date | Terms | Approx. Gross Proceeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| July 2025 Offering | Closed Jul 18, 2025 | 13,850,000 shares @ $3.25 | ~$45.0M gross (~$41.8M net) — *this one is a confirmed subsequent event per [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 17]* |
| October 2025 Registered Direct Offering | Oct 2025 | Up to 16,077,175 shares @ $9.34 | ~$150.2M |
| February 2026 Public Offering | Feb 2026 | 20,000,000 shares @ $5.00 | ~$100.0M |

Note the wide price dispersion across these raises ($3.25 → $9.34 → $5.00 per share within about seven months) — consistent with the stock's high volatility (52-week range $3.21–$12.58 per external sources) rather than a steady financing cadence at a stable valuation.

## 4. Share Count / Dilution Update

Shares outstanding grew from 58,491,196 (FYE2025, audited) to approximately 125,321,172 by February 6, 2026, and to 145,327,493–145,587,048 by March–May 2026 — roughly a **2.5x increase in under 12 months** (Note: from external source). This is the single most important data point to reconcile against the strong cash-balance headline: NioCorp funded essentially all of its liquidity improvement through primary equity issuance, not through the EXIM debt facility it has been pursuing since 2023 — the debt piece of its stated "two-thirds debt, one-third equity" funding plan [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 7] has not yet materialized.

## 5. Financing/Strategic Developments (per external reporting, not yet reflected in any audited filing)

- **DoD Agreement** confirmed as a subsequent event in the FY2025 10-K (signed August 4, 2025, up to $10.0M milestone-based reimbursement) [NB_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 17] — the earliest of these post-FYE2025 developments and the only one independently corroborated by the audited filing itself.
- **EXIM Bank:** reportedly advanced further in due diligence during early 2026, with CEO Mark Smith expressing confidence (per January 2026 external commentary) that full financing could close by end of Q2 2026 — no confirmed final commitment has been independently verified as of this report's August 4, 2026 research date (Note: from external source, Mining.com, niocorp.com, Crux Investor coverage, January–May 2026).
- **Traxys North America term sheet** (April 9, 2026): non-binding 10-year marketing/offtake term sheet covering remaining planned Elk Creek output, with an option for Traxys to invest up to $30 million in NioCorp equity (Note: from external source, StockTitan, April 2026) — if this converts to a binding agreement, it would be the first disclosed step toward contracting the currently-uncommitted scandium/titanium production (see [[Bear Case]]).
- **Construction milestone:** mine portal construction reportedly launched in February 2026, with a ~$44.6 million budget for twin mine ramp excavation, access road, and site infrastructure; an MSHA "Notice of Commencement" was filed (Note: from external source, Investing News Network, Nebraska Public Media, niocorp.com, February–March 2026). If accurate, this would represent the company's first physical construction activity — a meaningful, tangible step beyond financing and permitting work, though it should be verified against the primary 10-Q/8-K filings rather than relied upon solely from secondary reporting.

## 6. What Changed (Analyst Synthesis)

The three quarters since the FY2025 10-K appear to represent the most consequential period in NioCorp's recent history: a ~16x increase in cash, a construction-milestone announcement, and continued (if still unconfirmed) progress on the EXIM financing application. But this progress has come at the cost of roughly 2.5x dilution in share count over the same period, and — critically — **none of it has been independently verified against a primary SEC filing for this report**, since only the FY2023-FY2025 10-Ks were downloaded and read directly. The going-concern question that has persisted for three straight audited fiscal years cannot be confirmed as resolved or unresolved without reading the actual FY2026 10-K (expected ~September 2026) or at minimum the Q3 FY2026 10-Q directly.

**Recommended follow-up:** download and directly review the March 31, 2026 10-Q and any subsequent 8-Ks referencing the EXIM Bank decision, the updated Feasibility Study, or the Traxys term sheet before finalizing any position sizing decision — this earnings note should be treated as directional context, not as a substitute for primary-source verification of the FY2026 developments.
