# Marvell Technology, Inc. (MRVL) — Financial Deep Dive

*Analysis date: 2026-07-02 | Primary source: Form 10-K FY2026 (fiscal year ended January 31, 2026, filed March 11, 2026), cross-checked against FY2025 and FY2024 10-Ks*

---

## Accounting Basis

MRVL reports under U.S. GAAP in USD millions. **No non-GAAP reconciliation table appears in any of the three 10-Ks reviewed** — Marvell's widely-cited non-GAAP figures (e.g., non-GAAP EPS) are disclosed only in earnings press releases/8-Ks, not the annual report itself. This is flagged explicitly per source-discipline rules: all figures below are GAAP as filed unless otherwise noted.

**The FY2026 swing to GAAP profitability is not primarily an operating story.** Operating income did improve substantially (from a $720.3M loss to a $1,322.9M profit), driven by revenue growth (+42.1% YoY) and margin expansion (gross margin 41.3%→51.0%) plus a sharp restructuring-charge decline. But the "Interest income and other, net" line jumped from $15.0M (FY2025) to $1,926.3M (FY2026), driven almost entirely by the **$1,830.4M pre-tax gain on the sale of the automotive ethernet business to Infineon** [MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm, Item 1] — a one-time, non-operating item. Excluding this gain, pre-tax income would have been roughly $1,216.2M rather than $3,046.6M, still a major improvement but a much smaller one.

## 4. Segment Profitability

MRVL operates as a **single reportable segment** for GAAP purposes [MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm, Notes / Note 14]: "the CODM is ultimately responsible for and actively involved in the allocation of resources and the assessment of the Company's performance using consolidated net income (loss)." No segment-level operating income or margin is disclosed — only revenue by end market (data center vs. communications and other, see [[Business Overview]]) and by customer type:

[MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm, Note 14]: "Direct customers $4,630.4 [million] 57% ... Distributors $3,564.2 43%" (FY2026).

**Revenue by geography (shipment destination):** [MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm, Note 14]: "China $2,969.9 36% $2,507.6 43% $2,371.0 43% Taiwan 1,657.3 20% 560.7 10% 161.9 3% United States 1,174.1 14% 956.9 17% 795.6 14%" (FY2026/FY2025/FY2024). Taiwan's share of shipment-destination revenue jumped from 3% (FY2024) to 20% (FY2026) — likely reflecting foundry/OSAT shipment logistics for AI chips rather than true end-customer geography, while China's share, though still large (36% in FY2026), has been declining as a percentage even as absolute dollars grew.

## 8. Going-Concern, Impairment, and One-Time Items

- **Going concern:** No qualification found in any of the three filings.
- **Goodwill impairment:** [MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm]: annual impairment test "did not result in any impairment charge" in FY2026, FY2025, or FY2024. Goodwill declined from $11,586.9M to $11,062.2M in FY2026 solely due to $524.7M derecognized as part of the automotive ethernet divestiture — not an impairment.
- **Restructuring — "Fiscal 2025 Plan":** [MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm, Note 4]: Restructuring charges were $131.1M (FY2024), $711.8M (FY2025, driven by the "Fiscal 2025 Plan" that shifted R&D investment toward data center), and $16.0M (FY2026). The FY2025 charge included a **$240.1M acquired intangible asset impairment**, $159.0M of impaired purchased technology licenses, $63.1M inventory write-off, $36.0M property/equipment impairment, and $25.2M other non-current asset impairment — substantial, acquisition-related write-downs tied to Marvell's strategic pivot away from legacy end markets and toward data center/AI.
- **One-time gain — automotive ethernet divestiture:** $1,830.4M pre-tax gain on the $2.5B cash sale to Infineon (completed August 14, 2025), the primary driver of FY2026's GAAP net income swing to positive (see Section 1).
- **Acquired intangible assets:** [MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm, Note 5]: Developed technologies $1,079.0M (FY2026) / $1,695.9M (FY2025); customer relationships $373.5M / $666.5M; trade names $2.2M / $12.2M; total amortizable $1,454.7M / $2,374.6M; plus IPR&D $300.0M / $336.0M; grand total $1,754.7M / $2,710.6M. Weighted-average remaining amortization period: 2.8 years. Amortization expense: $1,097.9M (FY2024) / $1,052.6M (FY2025) / $942.0M (FY2026) — a substantial, persistent non-cash expense reflecting Marvell's history of large, debt/equity-funded acquisitions (Inphi, Innovium, and others).
- **Subsequent events (not reflected in the FY2026 balance sheet):** Celestial AI acquisition (closed Feb 2, 2026, $1.3B cash / $1.0B net + 24.5M shares) and XConn Technologies acquisition (closed Feb 10, 2026, $280.0M cash + 2.1M shares) — both post-FYE, adding further goodwill/intangibles and share dilution not yet reflected in these financials.

Not found in available filings: a non-GAAP income statement reconciliation (Marvell's widely-cited non-GAAP EPS figures, e.g. the $0.80 non-GAAP EPS reported for Q1 FY2027 per external sources, do not appear in the 10-K itself).
