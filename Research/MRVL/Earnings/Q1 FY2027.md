## Guidance vs. Reality

Revenue of $2.418 billion was roughly in line with the ~$2.41 billion consensus estimate; non-GAAP EPS of $0.80 matched or slightly beat consensus depending on source. Management raised full-year guidance meaningfully: **FY2027 revenue now expected to grow ~40% YoY to approximately $11.5 billion**, and the **FY2028 revenue target was raised to approximately $16.5 billion** (up $1.5 billion from the prior guide) — an aggressive, multi-year growth commitment that stands well above the FY2026 10-K's disclosed FY2026 revenue of $8,194.6 million.

**Note on disclosure basis:** the 10-K reviewed for this research package (covering FYE January 31, 2026) does not contain a non-GAAP reconciliation table ([[Financial Deep Dive]]), so the $0.80 non-GAAP EPS figure above cannot be cross-checked against filed GAAP-to-non-GAAP bridge methodology — it is taken as reported in the earnings release/transcript coverage.

## KPI Trends

Data center now represents 76% of quarterly revenue, up from 74% for full-year FY2026 [MRVL_10K_FY2026.htm] — continuing the multi-year shift described in [[Business Overview]]. Management's commentary attributed growth to "exceptional demand" for AI solutions, and specifically cited **AWS Trainium as the largest single ASIC contributor to data center revenue**, with **Trainium 3 cited as ramping production starting Q2 2026** (Note: from external source).

This Trainium3 ramp commentary is notable in light of separate, unconfirmed external reporting (see [[Industry Scan]] and [[Bear Case]]) suggesting Trainium3 specifically went to a competitor (Alchip) rather than Marvell — the sources reviewed for this note do not resolve this apparent tension, and it should be treated as an open question requiring direct verification (e.g., from the next earnings call transcript or 10-Q) rather than assumed resolved in either direction.

## What Changed

1. **Guidance raised meaningfully for both FY2027 and FY2028** — a bullish signal on management's confidence in the near-term pipeline, though as with any forward guidance from a company with 82% customer concentration ([[Business Overview]]), the credibility of multi-year targets depends heavily on a small number of customer relationships continuing to perform.
2. **Record operating cash flow** ($638.8 million) confirms the data-center revenue growth is translating into real cash generation this quarter, not just GAAP accounting improvement (contrast with the FY2026 10-K, where GAAP net income was substantially inflated by the one-time automotive-ethernet divestiture gain, [[Financial Deep Dive]]).
3. **Data center revenue concentration continues to rise** (76% of Q1 FY2027 revenue vs. 74% for full-year FY2026) — the business is becoming more, not less, dependent on the AI/data-center category and, by extension, on the concentrated customer base within it.
4. **No update found** on the reported Trainium3 design-loss story specifically — the sources reviewed for this note cite Trainium3 as "ramping" with Marvell silicon, which is either inconsistent with the design-loss reporting cited elsewhere in this vault, or refers to a different aspect of the program (e.g., partial content rather than full-socket win). This should be the first item resolved when this research package is next updated.
