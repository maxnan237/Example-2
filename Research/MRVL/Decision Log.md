# Marvell Technology, Inc. (MRVL) — Decision Log

*Initiated: 2026-07-02*

---

## Investment Thesis Summary

MRVL is a fabless semiconductor company whose data-center/AI custom-silicon business has nearly tripled in two years (from $2,216.7M to $6,100.3M FY2024-FY2026), now 74% of total revenue, but the growth rests on an extremely concentrated and unnamed customer base (top 10 = 82% of revenue) in a design-win business model where a single lost program can foreclose years of revenue.

**Bull thesis:** Data-center revenue and gross margin (41.6%→51.0%) have both expanded dramatically; management has executed a disciplined portfolio pivot (divesting automotive ethernet at an attractive $2.5B valuation, redeploying into AI-interconnect M&A); the company's own technology capability (leading-edge process nodes, advanced packaging, 10,000+ patents) provides genuine differentiation; management has raised its FY2028 revenue target to ~$16.5B (external source).

**Bear thesis:** Customer concentration has worsened for five consecutive years, not improved; Marvell is the clear #2 (~25% share) behind Broadcom (~60% share) in custom AI silicon per external reporting; a reported (unconfirmed) Trainium3 design loss to Alchip raises real doubt about the durability of Marvell's largest disclosed customer relationship; the company's own risk factors flag hyperscaler in-sourcing as a threat to the entire third-party ASIC model; and the stock trades at a substantial premium multiple (forward P/E ~60x) to more diversified, more scaled peers (Broadcom ~23x, Nvidia ~20x) with essentially no margin for error.

## Decision Framework

**To buy at ~$272:**
- Confirmation that the Trainium3 design-win reporting is either inaccurate or an isolated event, not a pattern
- Evidence of customer-base diversification (declining, not rising, concentration in Distributor A/top 10)
- Continued data-center revenue growth at or above the ~40% FY2027 guide in the next reported quarter

**To avoid:**
- Confirmation of the Trainium3 loss combined with any signal it may extend to Trainium4 or other programs
- Continued share-count growth despite large buyback spend, indicating capital allocation is not translating to per-share value
- Broader AI-infrastructure multiple compression that MRVL's elevated valuation is disproportionately exposed to

**Current stance: Monitor (Speculative lean, given valuation asymmetry)**

MRVL's underlying technology and execution on the data-center pivot are genuinely impressive, but this is a name where the valuation (forward P/E ~60x, well above Broadcom and Nvidia) already prices in continued, uninterrupted success across a small number of customer relationships that the company does not even name in its own filings. The combination of extreme customer concentration, an unconfirmed but credible design-loss report, and an explicit company-acknowledged risk that its own largest customers could disintermediate it entirely makes this a name to watch closely rather than buy at the current price — the risk/reward skews asymmetric to the downside per [[Valuation Check]], and the next 1-2 quarters of data-center growth and customer commentary should meaningfully clarify which thesis is playing out.

**Key triggers for position changes:**
- Next earnings report (expected ~August 2026) — does data-center growth and customer commentary confirm or dispel the Trainium3 design-loss concern?
- Any disclosure of customer-base diversification away from the current concentration levels
- Multiple compression toward peer levels (an entry opportunity) vs. further multiple expansion despite unresolved concentration risk (a reason for continued caution)
