# Constellation Software Inc. (CSU) — Decision Log

*Initiated: 2026-07-02*

---

## Investment Thesis Summary

CSU is a serial acquirer of vertical market software (VMS) businesses — ~150+ niche verticals, no customer over 2% of revenue, ~75% recurring maintenance revenue — trading near the cheapest relative multiple to its own history in years (down ~43.6% from its 52-week high), just as founder Mark Leonard steps down from the Board and a three-year acquisition-spend decline shows early signs of reversing.

**Bull thesis:** Genuinely diversified, switching-cost-protected portfolio of mission-critical software businesses with rising recurring-revenue mix and reaccelerating organic growth (0%→6% quarterly through FY2025); disciplined, decentralized capital allocation with forced manager equity reinvestment; static share count (no dilution) funded by disciplined debt issuance; trades at a comparable forward multiple to its closest peer (Roper Technologies) despite a longer, more proven track record; reported Q1/Q2 2026 acquisition deployment (~$1.6B) suggests the spending slowdown was opportunistic, not structural.

**Bear thesis:** Three consecutive years of declining acquisition cash spend ($1,609M→$1,347M→$1,227M) raise a real question about whether the acquisition pipeline is structurally shrinking; Mark Leonard's departure removes the founder credited with building CSU's disciplined culture, with unproven succession risk under new CEO Mark Miller; reported net income/EPS is unusually volatile due to large, recurring non-operating revaluation charges, making headline valuation multiples unreliable; rising competition for acquisition targets from private equity and other strategic roll-ups could compress historical acquisition economics.

## Decision Framework

**To buy at ~CAD $2,806:**
- Confirmation in the FY2026 annual results that acquisition spend has genuinely reaccelerated, not just in one anomalous quarter
- Evidence that Mark Miller is maintaining the same IRR-hurdle-rate discipline associated with Mark Leonard's tenure
- Continued organic growth reacceleration beyond the FY2025 exit rate

**To avoid:**
- Continued multi-year decline in acquisition cash spend despite ample available cash flow
- Any signal of relaxed capital-allocation discipline under new leadership
- Deceleration in organic growth reversing the FY2025 reacceleration trend

**Current stance: Monitor**

CSU's underlying portfolio quality is genuinely strong — this is not a business-model bear case in the way several other names in this vault are (e.g., MRVL's customer concentration, TME's competitive threat). The open questions are specific and checkable: whether the acquisition engine continues at pace under new leadership, and whether reported earnings volatility (from large, disclosed non-operating items) normalizes as expected. Both should become clearer with the next 1-2 quarters of results and the FY2026 annual filing (expected ~March 2027). Given the stock's sharp 52-week decline already reflects real uncertainty on these questions, this is a name to watch for confirmation rather than either buy or avoid outright at the current price.

**Key triggers for position changes:**
- FY2026 annual results (expected ~March 2027) confirming or refuting the acquisition-spend reacceleration
- Mark Miller's first full year of public capital-allocation commentary and track record
- Continued organic growth trend in quarterly MD&A disclosures
- Resolution/normalization of the large non-operating revaluation items affecting reported EPS
