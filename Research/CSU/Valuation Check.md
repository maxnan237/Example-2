## Key Valuation Considerations

CSU's headline trailing P/E (55-57x) is materially distorted by large, disclosed non-operating revaluation charges in FY2025 — investors relying on that figure alone would meaningfully overstate how expensive the stock actually is on a normalized operating basis. A cash-flow-based approach (FCFA2S) provides a cleaner lens, and on that basis CSU trades at a valuation broadly comparable to its closest peer, Roper Technologies, once forward multiples are used. The 52-week price range (CAD $2,196-$5,060, a peak-to-trough move of over 50%) reflects genuine uncertainty about both the AI-driven re-rating/de-rating of vertical software generally and CSU's own leadership transition specifically — this is a name where the market has already demonstrated it will move sharply on both fronts.

What would change the view: (1) confirmation that the FY2023-FY2025 acquisition-spend decline was opportunistic rather than structural (the reported ~$1.6B Q1/Q2 2026 deployment, external source, is an early positive signal but unconfirmed in filings), (2) evidence that Mark Miller's leadership is sustaining the same IRR-hurdle-rate discipline Mark Leonard is credited with establishing, (3) continued organic growth reacceleration beyond the FY2025 exit rate, and (4) resolution of the large one-time-item volatility in reported earnings, which should normalize once the Asseco/IRGA-related revaluations cycle through.
