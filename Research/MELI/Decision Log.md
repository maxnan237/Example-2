# MercadoLibre, Inc. (MELI) — Decision Log

*Initiated: 2026-07-02*

---

## Investment Thesis Summary

MELI is the dominant e-commerce and fintech platform in Latin America — 121M unique active buyers, $65.0B GMV, 78M fintech MAUs — facing its first genuinely direct test of competitive and margin resilience in years, just as founder Marcos Galperin hands the CEO role to internal successor Ariel Szarfsztejn.

**Bull thesis:** Regional market leadership across both commerce and fintech; fintech (the faster-growing segment) continues gaining share of the revenue mix; strong absolute cash flow generation (FCF more than doubled over two years to $10.8B); disciplined capital allocation with no dilution and conservative parent-level debt management; credible internal CEO succession; sell-side consensus still rates the stock Buy with ~26% implied upside.

**Bear thesis:** Brazil's segment margin declined YoY in FY2025 despite strong revenue growth — direct evidence of competitive pressure from Amazon, Shopee, and Temu; the credit business's NIMAL has compressed from 36.2% to 22.4% over two years as the loan book has nearly doubled annually; the company's own risk factors explicitly state barriers to entry are low; four consecutive quarterly EPS misses (external source) suggest the market's confidence in near-term margin recovery is genuinely shaken; extreme three-country revenue concentration (95.6%) with Argentina's history of severe currency volatility.

## Decision Framework

**To buy at ~$1,763:**
- Confirmation over the next 1-2 quarters that Brazil's segment margin has stabilized or begun recovering
- NIMAL stabilization signaling the credit book's marginal economics have bottomed
- Evidence new CEO Szarfsztejn is maintaining capital-allocation and growth discipline

**To avoid:**
- Continued Brazil segment margin decline alongside persistent competitive share loss
- Further NIMAL deterioration or a spike in provision for doubtful accounts beyond loan-book growth
- Renewed Argentina currency/inflation volatility given its 20.6% revenue share

**Current stance: Monitor**

MELI remains the clear regional leader with strong absolute growth, but this is a name where the bear case is directly supported by filed financial evidence (Brazil segment margin compression, declining NIMAL), not merely external speculation — a materially different situation from names like AVGO or UBER elsewhere in this vault where the moat has shown no comparable erosion signal. The premium valuation leaves limited room for continued disappointment, and the new CEO's first full year in the role, combined with the next 1-2 quarters of segment-level margin data, should meaningfully clarify whether the FY2025 pressure was a temporary competitive response or a more durable repricing of the business.

**Key triggers for position changes:**
- Next 1-2 quarters' segment-level margin data (especially Brazil)
- NIMAL trend in upcoming quarterly disclosures
- Confirmation of Szarfsztejn's strategic priorities as CEO
- Resolution of pending Mexico/Argentina banking license applications
