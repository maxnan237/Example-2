## Key Valuation Considerations

MELI's premium valuation (46.6x trailing P/E, second-highest among direct peers) has historically been justified by its dual-engine growth profile and regional market leadership, but Q1 2026 results (external source: revenue beat, EPS missed by ~12%, fourth consecutive quarterly profit miss) suggest the market is increasingly questioning whether that premium is still earned. The FY2025 10-K provides direct, filed evidence supporting this concern: Brazil's segment direct contribution margin declined YoY despite strong revenue growth, and the credit business's NIMAL has compressed from 36.2% to 22.4% over two years even as the loan book has nearly doubled annually.

What would change the view: (1) evidence in the next 1-2 quarters that Brazil's segment margin has stabilized or recovered, (2) NIMAL stabilization as the credit book matures, (3) confirmation that new CEO Ariel Szarfsztejn is maintaining the same growth-and-reinvestment capital allocation philosophy, and (4) continued positive Argentina macro developments under the current FX-band regime, given the country's outsized 20.6% share of consolidated revenue.
