# MELI Investment Memo

**MercadoLibre, Inc. (NASDAQ: MELI) | 2026-07-02**

---

## The Business in 30 Seconds

MercadoLibre is the leading e-commerce and fintech platform in Latin America — 121 million unique active buyers, $65.0 billion in GMV, and 78 million fintech monthly active users — operating across 18 countries in commerce and 8 in fintech, with 95.6% of revenue concentrated in Brazil, Argentina, and Mexico [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1].

---

## What's Going Right

**Strong absolute growth across every headline metric.** Revenue grew 39.1% YoY to $28,893 million in FY2025 [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm]; GMV, TPV, and fintech MAU all grew at double-digit-to-triple-digit rates.

**Fintech continues gaining share of the mix.** Fintech revenue grew 46.2% YoY versus 34.0% for Commerce [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 7] — the faster-growing segment is becoming a larger share of the business.

**Strong and accelerating free cash flow.** FCF more than doubled from $4,631M (FY2023) to $10,773M (FY2025) [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm].

**Disciplined capital structure.** No dilution since the 2028 convertible notes retired in November 2023; conservative, opportunistic parent-level debt issuance (new $750M 2033 Notes issued December 2025 alongside repaying the maturing 2026 Notes) [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 16].

**Credible internal CEO succession.** (Note: from external source) Ariel Szarfsztejn, with MELI since 2017 across strategy/logistics/marketplace roles, became CEO January 1, 2026; founder Marcos Galperin remains involved as Executive Chairman.

---

## What Could Go Wrong

**Brazil's segment margin declined YoY despite strong revenue growth.** Direct contribution fell from $2,286M to $2,075M (FY2024→FY2025) even as Brazil revenue grew 33.3% [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 8] — direct financial evidence of competitive margin pressure in MELI's largest market (52.6% of revenue).

**The credit business's per-dollar profitability has compressed sharply.** NIMAL fell from 36.2% (FY2023) to 22.4% (FY2025) even as the loan book has nearly doubled annually [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 7] — provision for doubtful accounts grew 194% over the same period, now 10.7% of total revenue.

**The company's own risk factors acknowledge low barriers to entry.** [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1A]: "Barriers-to-entry for large, well-established technology companies are relatively low, and current and new competitors can launch new sites at a relatively low cost using commercially available software" — an unusually direct, self-disclosed admission that undercuts a durable-moat narrative.

**Extreme geographic/currency concentration.** [MELI_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 1A]: Brazil, Argentina, and Mexico represent 95.6% of revenue; Argentina alone saw 211.4% inflation and 356.3% currency depreciation as recently as FY2023.

**Four consecutive quarterly EPS misses.** (Note: from external source) Q1 2026 revenue beat consensus by ~6.8% but EPS missed by ~12.2%, with the stock falling ~11-13% on the print — a persistent, not one-off, margin disappointment pattern.

**Named competitive pressure not disclosed in the company's own filings.** (Note: from external source) Amazon's reported >$10 billion Brazil investment and Shopee's rapid share gains (~15% of Brazilian e-commerce GMV, from near-zero in 2020) do not appear anywhere in MELI's three 10-Ks reviewed, despite being externally cited as the primary drivers of recent margin pressure.

## Decision

**Current stance: Monitor**

MercadoLibre remains the clear regional leader in Latin American e-commerce and fintech, with strong absolute growth in users, GMV, and cash flow. But this is a name where the bear case is directly supported by the company's own filed financial evidence — Brazil's segment margin compressed even as revenue grew strongly, and the credit business's per-dollar profitability has fallen by roughly a third over two years while the loan book has nearly doubled annually — not merely by external speculation about competitive pressure. Layer on extreme three-country revenue concentration, Argentina's history of severe currency volatility, a premium valuation that leaves little room for continued disappointment, and an unproven new CEO succeeding a two-decade founder-led tenure, and this is a name to watch for confirmation of margin stabilization over the next 1-2 quarters rather than either a clear buy or a clear avoid at the current price.

**Key triggers to watch:**
- Next 1-2 quarters' segment-level margin data, especially Brazil
- NIMAL trend and provision-for-doubtful-accounts trajectory in upcoming disclosures
- Confirmation of new CEO Ariel Szarfsztejn's strategic priorities and capital-allocation approach
- Resolution of pending Mexico and Argentina banking license applications

**Key risks to monitor:**
- Structural (not temporary) Brazil margin compression from Amazon/Shopee/Temu competition
- Credit-portfolio asset-quality deterioration as newer loan vintages season
- Renewed Argentina currency/inflation volatility given its 20.6% revenue share
- Continued quarterly EPS misses eroding market confidence in the premium multiple
