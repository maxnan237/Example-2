# McDonald's Corporation (MCD) — Business Overview

*Analysis date: 2026-07-02 | Primary source: Form 10-K (fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, filed February 24, 2026)*

*Note: MCD's 10-K uses a non-traditional SEC layout — sections are labeled descriptively (e.g., "Business Summary," "Risk Factors") rather than by Item number, with a cross-reference index mapping to traditional Item numbers. Citations below use the descriptive section names as they appear in the filing.*

## 1. What the Company Does

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Business Summary — Description of the Business]: "The Company franchises and owns and operates McDonald's restaurants, which serve a locally relevant menu of quality food and beverages in communities across more than 100 countries. Of the 45,356 McDonald's restaurants at year-end 2025, approximately 95% were franchised." → MCD is fundamentally a global franchisor, not primarily an operator.

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Business Summary]: "The Company is primarily a franchisor and believes franchising is paramount to delivering great-tasting food, locally relevant customer experiences and driving profitability... Directly operating McDonald's restaurants contributes significantly to the Company's ability to act as a credible franchisor." → Company-operated restaurants function as a "test kitchen"/proof-of-concept, not the core profit driver.

- **Employees:** Over 150,000 Company employees worldwide (~70% outside the U.S.); separately, "over two million individuals work in McDonald's franchised restaurants" as franchisee employees, not MCD Corp employees [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Human Capital Management]
- **HQ:** Chicago, Illinois (implied from standard MCD disclosure; not independently re-verified in this extraction)
- **Fiscal year end:** December 31
- **Restaurants:** 45,356 (2025) / 43,477 (2024) / 41,822 (2023), ~95% franchised system-wide

**Strategy:** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Strategic Direction]: "The Company's Accelerating the Arches growth strategy... encompasses all aspects of McDonald's business... growth pillars, M-C-D: Maximize our Marketing, Commit to the Core [menu], Double Down on the 4D's: Digital, Delivery, Drive Thru and Restaurant Development."

## 2. Business Segments

MCD reports three segments:

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Segment and Geographic Information]: "U.S. segment - the Company's largest market. The segment is 95% franchised... International Operated Markets segment - comprised of markets... including Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Poland, Spain and the U.K. The segment is 89% franchised... International Developmental Licensed Markets & Corporate - comprised primarily of developmental licensee and affiliate markets... including equity method investments in China and Japan... ## 3. Revenue Model

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Business Summary]: "The Company's revenues consist of sales by Company-owned and operated restaurants and fees from franchised restaurants operated by conventional franchisees, developmental licensees and affiliates... These fees, along with occupancy and operating rights, are stipulated in franchise/license agreements that generally have 20-year terms."

**Conventional franchise (primary U.S./IOM model):** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Business Summary — Conventional Franchise]: "the Company generally owns or secures a long-term lease on the land and building for the restaurant location and the franchisee pays for equipment, signs, seating and décor... Conventional franchisees contribute to the Company's revenue, primarily through the payment of rent and royalties based upon a percent of sales, with specified minimum rent payments, along with initial fees paid upon the opening of a new restaurant."

**Developmental license/affiliate (used for China, Japan, and most IDL markets):** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Business Summary]: "licensees are responsible for operating and managing their businesses, providing capital (including the real estate interest)... The Company generally does not invest any restaurant capital under a developmental license or affiliate arrangement, and it receives a royalty based on a percent of sales."

## 4. Customer Base & Concentration

MCD is a mass-consumer business with no disclosed customer concentration (thousands of independent franchisees, hundreds of millions of end consumers). Key digital/engagement metrics disclosed as **forward-looking targets, not current actuals**:

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Strategic Direction — Digital]: "The Company has loyalty programs in 70 markets... the Company plans to increase its 90-day active users to 250 million by the end of 2027... plans to grow its annual Systemwide sales to loyalty members to $45.0 billion by the end of 2027." → These are 2027 targets; the filing does not disclose the current actual 90-day active user count or current loyalty sales figure.

(Note: from external source, restauranttechnologynews.com, citing the Q4 2025 earnings call) Loyalty program reached 210 million 90-day active users across 70 markets, with loyalty-member sales nearly doubling in 2025 — this is external commentary, not a figure disclosed in the 10-K itself.

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Strategic Direction — Delivery]: "The Company offers delivery from nearly 41,000 restaurants across approximately 100 markets, representing approximately 90% of McDonald's restaurants."

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Strategic Direction — Drive Thru]: "The Company has the most drive thru locations worldwide, with nearly 29,000 drive thru locations globally, including over 95% of the approximately 13,700 locations in the U.S."

Not found in available filings: the brand name "MyMcDonald's Rewards" does not appear in these three 10-Ks; no disclosed digital-sales-as-%-of-total-systemwide-sales figure.

## 6. Key Dependencies

1. **Franchisee financial health and cooperation.** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Risk Factors]: "Our success as a heavily franchised business relies to a large degree on the financial success and cooperation of our franchisees... The revenues we realize from franchised restaurants are largely dependent on the ability of our franchisees to grow their sales." A 95%-franchised system means MCD's revenue is structurally a function of thousands of independent operators' performance.

2. **Real estate control as a franchise-renewal lever.** MCD owns ~56% of land and ~80% of buildings in consolidated markets [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Restaurant Development and Capital Expenditures] — a declining trend from 57% land ownership in 2023, worth monitoring.

3. **Brand and menu relevance.** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Risk Factors]: "Failure to preserve the value or relevance of our brand could have an adverse impact on our financial results."

4. **Global supply chain.** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Supply Chain, Food Safety and Quality]: "The Company and its franchisees purchase food, packaging, equipment, and other goods from numerous independent suppliers... The Company has a comprehensive strategy that its global supply chain organization leverages to identify, assess, and manage risk."

5. **Consumer discretionary spending, particularly among lower-income households.** (Note: from external source, PYMNTS/Baking Business) CEO Chris Kempczinski has stated consumer sentiment "certainly not improving, and it may be getting a little bit worse," with low-income consumer traffic down double digits — a demand-side dependency not quantified in the 10-K itself but material to the current investment picture.
