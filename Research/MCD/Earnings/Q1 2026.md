## Guidance vs. Reality

Q1 2026 results beat consensus on both the top and bottom line, with broad-based comparable-sales growth across all three segments (U.S. +3.9%, IOM +3.9%, IDL +3.4%) — a stronger and more even performance than the FY2025 full-year comps documented in [[Business Overview]] (U.S. +2.1%, IOM +3.2%, IDL +4.6%), suggesting some acceleration in the U.S. segment specifically into early 2026.

**Forward guidance:** management guided to a "meaningful deceleration" in Q2 2026 comparable sales, attributed to a difficult April year-over-year comparison. This is a specific, checkable forward statement — Q2 2026 results are due **July 29, 2026**, after this report's analysis date, and should be the first item checked when updating this research package.

## What Changed

1. **Broad-based comp-sales beat across all three segments** — a more even performance than the FY2025 full-year pattern (where IDL/Japan led and U.S. lagged), potentially reflecting early traction from value-menu initiatives ahead of the June 2026 "McDonald's > NEXT" strategy launch (Note: from external source, Axios).
2. **Management pre-announced a deceleration** for Q2, effectively setting a lower bar — worth watching whether the actual Q2 print beats this lowered bar (a positive signal) or merely meets it (neutral) or misses even the guided-down level (a negative signal, and a potential confirmation of the [[Bear Case]] demand-softness thesis).
3. **No update found on the ROIC trend** ([[Financial Deep Dive]]: 25.2%→21.8%→20.3% over FY2023-FY2025) — this metric is typically only disclosed annually in the 10-K, so Q1 2026 data does not yet confirm or refute whether the two-year decline is continuing into FY2026.
