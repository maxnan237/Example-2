## Key Valuation Considerations

MCD is a rare case of a wide-moat, Dividend King-status compounder trading near 52-week lows and at a discount to most healthy QSR peers — but the discount appears earned rather than purely sentiment-driven: after-tax ROIC has fallen from 25.2% to 20.3% over two years, buyback execution slowed sharply in FY2025 despite a large new authorization, and management itself has acknowledged softening low-income consumer demand. The 1-year stock performance of approximately -10% against an S&P 500 return of approximately +30% (Note: from external source) reflects the market pricing these concerns more severely than the underlying business fundamentals (still-positive comparable sales, continued dividend growth, Q1 2026 beat) would suggest on their own.

What would change the view: (1) Q2 2026 results (due July 29, 2026) showing the guided deceleration was conservative rather than the start of a deeper slowdown, (2) evidence the "McDonald's > NEXT" value strategy is stabilizing low-income traffic, (3) a return to the FY2023-level buyback pace signaling management sees the current price as attractive, and (4) stabilization or reversal of the two-year ROIC decline.
