**MCD's total shareholders' equity remains negative in all three years shown** — a deficit narrowing from $(4,707)M (FY2023) to $(3,797)M (FY2024) to $(1,791)M (FY2025), driven by retained-earnings growth outpacing treasury stock buybacks. This is a structural feature of MCD's capital structure (decades of aggressive buybacks against a modest asset base relative to earnings power), not a going-concern signal — no going-concern language appears anywhere in the three filings reviewed.

## 7. Dividend History

[MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, MD&A]: "The Company has paid dividends on its common stock for 50 consecutive years and has increased the dividend amount every year. The 2025 full year dividend of $7.17 per share reflects the quarterly dividend paid for each of the first three quarters of $1.77 per share, with an increase to $1.86 per share paid in the fourth quarter. This 5% increase in the quarterly dividend equates to a $7.44 per share annual dividend." → MCD is a confirmed Dividend Aristocrat/King with 50 consecutive years of increases through FY2025.

## 8. Going-Concern, Impairment, and One-Time Items

- **Going concern:** No qualification found in any of the three filings.
- **Goodwill impairment:** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Note "Goodwill"]: "In the current period, the Company performed a qualitative assessment and did not identify any indicators of impairment. Historically, goodwill impairment has not significantly impacted the Consolidated Financial Statements." Goodwill grew from $3,145M (2024) to $3,354M (2025), largely via currency translation ($140M) and restaurant purchases.
- **Restructuring — "Accelerating the Organization" initiative:** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm, Note "Other Operating (Income) Expense, Net"]: "In 2025, impairment and other charges (gains), net reflected pre-tax charges of $229 million, primarily related to restructuring charges associated with the Company's internal effort to modernize ways of working (Accelerating the Organization). In 2024, impairment and other charges (gains), net reflected pre-tax charges of $221 million... and net pre-tax charges of $70 million, primarily consisting of transaction costs, property sale gains and non-cash impairment charges associated with the sale of McDonald's business in South Korea and transaction costs associated with the acquisition of McDonald's business in Israel." 2023: "$290 million of pre-tax charges related to the Company's Accelerating the Arches growth strategy... and $72 million of pre-tax charges related to the write-off of impaired software no longer in use."
- **Declining ROIC — worth flagging:** [MCD_10K_FY2025.htm]: "After-tax return on invested capital from continuing operations... was 20.3%, 21.8% and 25.2% as of December 31, 2025, 2024, and 2023, respectively." A ~490bp decline in ROIC over two years despite growing absolute operating income — indicates capital deployed (rising capex, the increased Grand Foods Holding equity-method stake) is generating lower incremental returns than the existing asset base, a trend worth watching in [[Bear Case]] and [[Decision Log]].
- **M&A / investing activity:** FY2024 cash flow shows a $1,837M "Purchases of equity method investments" outflow, described in the MD&A as "primarily due to the Company's increased ownership stake in Grand Foods Holding in 2024." Restaurant-business purchases: $441M (2023) / $669M (2024) / $354M (2025).

Not found in available filings: FY2023 goodwill balance broken out by segment (only the FY2024/FY2025 rollforward by segment was extracted).
