# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) — Financial Deep Dive

*Analysis date: 2026-07-02 | Primary source: Form 10-K FY2025 (fiscal year ended June 30, 2025, filed July 2025), cross-checked against FY2024 and FY2023 10-Ks*

---

## Accounting Basis

MSFT reports under U.S. GAAP in USD millions.

**"Other income (expense), net" has been deteriorating** — from +$788M (FY2023) to -$1,646M (FY2024) to -$4,901M (FY2025) — a trend worth monitoring, though the filings reviewed do not provide a detailed line-item breakdown of this deterioration within the extracted material.

## 2. Cash Flow Statement (USD million)

[MSFT_10K_FY2025.htm, Cash Flows Statements]:

| $M | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net cash from operations | 87,582 | 118,548 | 136,162 |
| Capex (additions to PP&E) | 28,107 | 44,477 | 64,551 |
| **Free cash flow (calculated: OpCF − Capex)** | **59,475** | **74,071** | **71,611** |
| Net cash used in investing | (22,680) | (96,970) | (72,599) |
| Common stock repurchased | (22,245) | (17,254) | (18,420) |
| Dividends paid | (19,800) | (21,771) | (24,082) |
| Net cash used in financing | (43,935) | (37,757) | (51,699) |

**Capex has more than doubled in two years** — from $28.1B (FY2023) to $64.6B (FY2025), a 45% YoY increase in FY2025 alone. [MSFT_10K_FY2025.htm, Item 7]: the filing attributes this to "cloud and AI investments" in qualitative terms but does not provide a discrete, segmented dollar breakdown of AI-specific vs. general cloud capex — not found in available filings as a quoted figure.

**FCF actually declined YoY in FY2025** ($74,071M → $71,611M) despite operating cash flow growing $17.6bn, because capex growth ($20.1bn) outpaced the operating cash flow gain. This is the clearest filing-level evidence of the capex/cash-flow tension that (per [[Valuation Check]]) has driven much of MSFT's 2026 stock decline.

## 6. Share Count & Buyback Program

[MSFT_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 15]: "Balance, beginning of year 7,434 7,432 7,464 [millions]... Balance, end of year 7,434 7,434 7,432" (this appears to be presenting FY2025/FY2024/FY2023 activity; diluted share counts per the income statement were 7,465M/7,469M/7,472M for FY2025/24/23 respectively).

| | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shares repurchased (M) | 69 | 32 | 31 |
| Buyback spend, gross ($M) | 18,400 | 11,960 | 13,000 |
| Buyback cash outflow, per cash flow statement ($M) | 22,245 | 17,254 | 18,420 |

[MSFT_10K_FY2025.htm]: Employee tax-withholding share settlements added "$5.4 billion, $5.3 billion, and $3.8 billion for fiscal years 2025, 2024, and 2023" to the total cash outflow, explaining the gap between the "gross buyback" and "cash flow statement" figures above.

Diluted share count has declined modestly and consistently (7,472M→7,469M→7,465M), confirming buybacks have been outpacing dilution — a contrast with some other companies in this research vault (e.g., MRVL) where dilution has outpaced buybacks.

## 7. Dividend History

[MSFT_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 15]: FY2025 quarterly dividends of $0.83/share each (total $3.32/share, $24,678M declared); FY2024 quarterly dividends of $0.75/share (total $3.00/share, $22,295M declared); [MSFT_10K_FY2023.htm]: FY2023 quarterly dividends of $0.68/share (total $2.72/share, $20,233M declared).

**Dividend growth:** $0.62/qtr (FY2022, prior-year baseline) → $0.68 (FY2023, +9.7%) → $0.75 (FY2024, +10.3%) → $0.83 (FY2025, +10.7%) — a consistent, accelerating double-digit dividend growth rate, funded comfortably by free cash flow of $71.6bn against dividends paid of $24.1bn (FY2025).

Note: cash actually paid per the cash flow statement ($24,082M FY2025) differs slightly from the "declared" total ($24,678M) due to timing between declaration and payment dates.

## 8. Going-Concern, Impairment, and One-Time Items

- **Going concern:** No qualification found in any of the three filings.
- **Goodwill impairment:** [MSFT_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 8]: "No instances of impairment were identified in our May 1, 2025, May 1, 2024, or May 1, 2023 tests. As of June 30, 2025 and 2024, accumulated goodwill impairment was $11.3 billion" — this is a legacy, historical cumulative balance (largely from pre-2015 acquisitions), not a current-year charge.
- **FY2023 restructuring ("Q2 charge"):** [MSFT_10K_FY2023.htm, Item 7]: "On January 18, 2023, we announced decisions we made to align our cost structure with our revenue and customer demand... we recorded a $1.2 billion charge in the second quarter of fiscal year 2023... which included employee severance expenses of $800 million, impairment charges resulting from changes to our hardware portfolio, and costs related to lease consolidation activities... we reduced our overall workforce by approximately 10,000 jobs." Quantified impact: "gross margin, operating income, net income, and diluted EPS were negatively impacted by the Q2 charge, which resulted in decreases of $152 million, $1.2 billion, $946 million, and $0.13, respectively." **No equivalent restructuring charge is disclosed in the FY2024 or FY2025 filings** — a one-time FY2023 event, not a recurring pattern.
- **Intangible asset amortization (elevated due to Activision):** [MSFT_10K_FY2025.htm, Note 9]: "$6.0 billion, $4.8 billion, and $2.5 billion for fiscal years 2025, 2024, and 2023, respectively" — a growing non-cash expense tied to the Activision Blizzard acquisition.

Not found in available filings: a discrete, quoted dollar breakdown of AI-specific vs. general cloud capex within the reported "Additions to property and equipment" line.
