# Tencent Music Entertainment Group (TME) — Financial Deep Dive

*Analysis date: 2026-07-02 | Primary source: Form 20-F FY2025 (filed April 17, 2026), cross-checked against FY2024 and FY2023 20-Fs*

---

## Accounting Basis (Important Note)

TME reports under **IFRS Accounting Standards as issued by the IASB — not U.S. GAAP.** [TME_20F_FY2025.htm, cover page]: "International Financial Reporting Standards as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board ☑" is the box checked, U.S. GAAP is unchecked. Terminology on the face of the statements ("Profit for the year," "Operating profit") is IFRS-native.

**Currency:** Primary statements are presented in RMB million; USD figures are convenience translations only. FY2025 filing uses the December 31, 2025 noon buying rate of **RMB6.9931 = US$1.00**. Prior-year translations: RMB7.2993 = US$1.00 (Dec 31, 2024); RMB7.0999 = US$1.00 (Dec 31, 2023).

## 8. Going-Concern, Impairment, and One-Time Items

- **Going concern:** No qualification found in any of the three filings — only standard capital-management boilerplate.
- **Goodwill impairment:** [TME_20F_FY2025.htm, Note 16]: "No impairment had been recognized for the years ended December 31, 2023, 2024 and 2025." Goodwill of ~RMB17.8bn "mainly arose from the Merger in 2016" and the long-form audio acquisition.
- **Minor equity-method impairment:** [TME_20F_FY2025.htm, Note 17]: "recognized an impairment provision of RMB64 million and RMB82 million for the years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025, respectively" on an associate investment — immaterial.
- **One-time item — FY2025 "Other gains, net" spike:** Jumped from RMB165m (2024) to RMB2,632m (2025), driven by RMB2,285m of net investment gains per the Non-IFRS reconciliation. This materially inflates the reported FY2025 operating-profit growth rate and should be excluded when assessing underlying operating momentum (use the Non-IFRS adjusted profit figures instead).
- **Pending Ximalaya acquisition (not yet consolidated in these financials):** [TME_20F_FY2025.htm, Item 4.A]: cash consideration of US$1.26 billion plus Class A shares — a capital commitment not reflected in the FY2025 balance sheet as of the filing date, since the deal had not closed.

Not found in available filings: a reconciled explanation for a small (~US$8m) discrepancy between the Item 5 MD&A-cited FY2025 buyback spend (US$64m) and the RMB390m cash-flow-statement figure at the FY2025 average FX rate — likely a trade-date vs. settlement-date timing difference, not separately explained in the filing text.
