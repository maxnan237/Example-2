## KPI Trends — Material Disclosure Change

**TME discontinued quarterly disclosure of online music MAU, paying users, and ARPPU starting this quarter (Q1 2026).** Going forward, the company states it will report paying users on an annual basis only. This is a significant reduction in monitoring transparency for the single metric this research package identifies as the central open question in the investment thesis (see [[Moat Analysis]] and [[Bear Case]]) — investors can no longer track quarter-to-quarter whether the FY2023-FY2025 MAU decline (589m → 570m → 547m, per [TME_20F_FY2025.htm, Item 5]) is stabilizing or accelerating until the next annual filing.

For context, the last quarter with full KPI disclosure was Q4 2025 (reported ~March 17, 2026): MAU declined 5% YoY to a reported 528M; paying users rose 5.3% YoY to 127.4M; SVIP subscribers exceeded 20M; ARPPU rose 7% YoY to RMB11.9. That release triggered a ~20.8-25% single-session stock decline despite otherwise solid revenue/EPS results, driven by market concern over the MAU decline and competition from ByteDance's "Soda Music" app (external reporting; not named in TME's own SEC filings).

---

## What Changed

1. **KPI transparency reduced.** The move to annual-only user-metric disclosure is the single most consequential change this quarter for how this research package should be maintained going forward — the [[Decision Log]] watch-list item on MAU trajectory can now only be updated annually, not quarterly.
2. **Segment mix continued shifting toward music.** Music-related services revenue (+12.2% YoY) continues to outgrow the shrinking social entertainment segment (-11.0% YoY), consistent with the multi-year trend documented in [[Business Overview]] and [[Financial Deep Dive]].
3. **Profitability growth moderated versus the outsized FY2025 annual figure.** Non-IFRS net profit grew "only" 7% YoY this quarter, versus the ~22% YoY growth in FY2025 adjusted profit for the full year — a reminder that the FY2025 annual growth rate was flattered by a one-time investment gain (see [[Financial Deep Dive]], Section 5), and the underlying quarterly run-rate growth is more modest.
4. **No new guidance on the Ximalaya integration's financial contribution** was found in the sources reviewed for this note — a gap to track in future quarters as the deal (closed May 18, 2026, per external reporting) begins contributing to consolidated results.
