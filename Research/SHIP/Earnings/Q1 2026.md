## KPI Trends

**TCE outperformance versus the benchmark index is a genuinely positive signal.** SHIP achieved $24,219/day against a BCI-180 average of $22,902/day for the same period — a 6% premium, suggesting either favorable charter timing, the fixed-floor-plus-profit-share structure on two vessels, or effective commercial management. This is a useful, filing-independent data point to track each quarter as a measure of whether SHIP is capturing value above the raw index it is contractually tied to.

The swing from a net loss (Q1 2025: -$6.8M GAAP) to solid profitability (Q1 2026: $9.7M GAAP) reflects the broader TCE recovery from the FY2025 full-year average of $20,937/day toward the $24,219/day achieved this quarter — consistent with the BCI's reported Q2 2026 strength (+25%, per external sources in [[Industry Scan]]), though the index's high volatility (a ~22% June 2026 decline) means this recovery should not be assumed durable without confirmation in the next quarter's results.

## What Changed

1. **Dividend continuity confirmed.** The company declared its 18th consecutive quarterly dividend of $0.20/share (payable ~July 10, 2026), with cumulative distributions of $2.84/share since program inception (Note: from external source) — a continuation of the variable, cycle-linked policy described in [[Financial Deep Dive]], now at a level between the FY2024 peak and FY2025 trough quarterly rates.
2. **Rate environment improved materially quarter-over-quarter** relative to the FY2025 full-year average, though it remains below the FY2024 peak ($25,063/day) — a partial, not complete, recovery.
3. **No update found** on the FY2025-disclosed working capital deficit ($13,171K as of Dec 31, 2025) — the sources reviewed for this note did not confirm whether the post-year-end sale-and-leaseback arrangements referenced in the 20-F have resolved this item.
4. **No update found** on the newbuilding program's status (on-schedule confirmation, any delivery date changes) in the sources reviewed for this earnings note specifically.
