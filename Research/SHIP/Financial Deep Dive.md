Vessels, net of $506,542K against total debt of ~$294,000K implies a loan-to-vessel-value ratio of roughly 58% — a moderate leverage level for a shipping company, though see the working capital note below.

**Working capital deficit flagged.** [SHIP_20F_FY2025.htm, Note 1]: the company discloses a **working capital deficit of $13,171K** as of December 31, 2025 (current liabilities of $88,062K exceeding current assets of $74,891K), attributed to debt repayments coming due within twelve months. Management states this will be addressed "via cash from operations and through... five sale and leaseback agreements entered into subsequent to year end" — not a going-concern qualification (none was issued), but a real, disclosed near-term liquidity item worth tracking.

**Accumulated deficit remains substantially negative** (-$319,464K as of FY2025) despite three consecutive years of positive net income — a legacy of the company's pre-2023 history (including the 1-for-10 reverse stock split driven by Nasdaq minimum-bid-price non-compliance), reflecting a business that required a genuine turnaround to reach current profitability.

See [[Business Overview]] Section 3 for the full TCE reconciliation table. Summary: TCE rate fell from $25,063/day (FY2024) to $20,937/day (FY2025), a 16% decline, while daily vessel operating expenses rose from $6,976 to $7,127/day over the same period — a genuine margin squeeze from both sides (falling revenue per day, rising cost per day) in FY2025.

| June 2022 Alpha Bank Facility | $12,000K | SOFR + 2.40% (reduced from 2.95% via Sept 2025 amendment) |
| October 2024 Alpha Bank Facility | $29,200K | SOFR + 2.40% |

Several older facilities (August 2021 Alpha Bank, Sinopac, June 2022 Piraeus, October 2022 Danish Ship Finance, December 2022 Alpha Bank) were fully repaid/refinanced during FY2025.

**Collateral:** [SHIP_20F_FY2025.htm, Note 8]: "ten of the Company's owned vessels, having a net carrying value of $264,062, were subject to first and second priority mortgages... In addition, the Company's ten bareboat chartered vessels, having a net carrying value of $242,480... have been financed through sale and leaseback agreements." → The fleet is essentially fully encumbered — either via mortgage or sale-leaseback — a standard but material structural feature of shipping finance.

**Maturity schedule (total $293,958K):** 2026 – $56,903K; 2027 – $59,243K; 2028 – $43,233K; 2029 – $50,519K; Thereafter – $84,060K.

**Financial covenants** include corporate leverage ratio caps (≤65-70%), security cover ratios (125-143%), and minimum liquidity requirements per borrower ($500K-$3,000K) [SHIP_20F_FY2025.htm, Note 8].

**Growing reliance on Chinese leasing companies.** Multiple FY2025 transactions were financed via sale-and-leaseback with Chinese lessors (Huarong, BOCL), alongside traditional Greek/Danish bank facilities — a diversified but increasingly leasing-heavy capital structure.

## 6. Share Count & Dilution

[SHIP_20F_FY2025.htm]: "the Company effected a one-for-ten reverse stock split of the Company's common stock" effective **February 16, 2023**, driven by Nasdaq minimum bid price non-compliance (Rule 5550(a)(2)) — regained compliance March 3, 2023.

| | Jan 1, 2023 | Dec 31, 2023 | Dec 31, 2024 | Dec 31, 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shares outstanding | 18,191,614 | 19,636,352 | 20,374,165 | 21,114,098 |

Shares outstanding grew steadily (~16% cumulative over the period) via an ATM offering program (up to $30,000K aggregate, agreement with B. Riley Securities, Dec 2023), stock-based compensation, and warrant exercises — a persistent, moderate dilution trend, partially offset by small buybacks in FY2023 ($1,679K) and FY2024 ($4,850K); no buybacks in FY2025.

**Governance note — super-voting preferred stock.** [SHIP_20F_FY2025.htm]: 20,000 Series B preferred shares (voting only, no dividend rights) were issued to CEO Stamatios Tsantanis in December 2021 for a total of $250, carrying 25,000 votes/share, capped at 49.99% aggregate voting power. This gives the CEO outsized, structurally-entrenched voting control disproportionate to his economic ownership — a material governance consideration (see [[Management Review]]).

**No warrants outstanding as of December 31, 2025** — Class E Warrants expired August 2025; a January 2023 tender offer had already repurchased ~47% of outstanding Class E warrants.

## 8. Going-Concern, Impairment, and One-Time Items

- **Going concern:** No formal qualification issued in any of the three filings, though the FY2025 filing discloses a working capital deficit of $13,171K (see Section 3) that management attributes to near-term debt maturities, addressed via post-year-end financing arrangements.
- **No vessel impairment charges recorded in any of FY2023, FY2024, or FY2025**, despite periodic impairment-indicator testing: [SHIP_20F_FY2023.htm]: "indicators of impairment existed for four of the Company's vessels" (aggregate carrying value $121,577K) — "no impairment charge should be recorded." [SHIP_20F_FY2024.htm]: indicators existed for one vessel — no charge. [SHIP_20F_FY2025.htm]: "no impairment loss should be recorded as of December 31, 2025 and 2024." This is a genuinely favorable track record through a period of declining TCE rates, though it also means any future rate downturn severe enough to trigger an actual impairment would represent a meaningful deterioration from the current trend.
- **One-time items:** Loss on extinguishment of debt each year ($1,663K FY2025 / $653K FY2024 / $540K FY2023) from facility refinancings; Gain on sale of vessels, net — related party ($8,094K, FY2023 only, related to the sale of the M/V Lordship); Gain on sale of vessel, net ($2,308K, FY2025, related to the Geniuship disposal).
- **Nasdaq compliance history:** the 1-for-10 reverse stock split (Feb 2023) was a direct response to a bid-price compliance failure — worth noting as part of the company's recent history, even though compliance was regained shortly after.
