# Seanergy Maritime Holdings Corp. (SHIP) — Decision Log

*Initiated: 2026-07-02*

---

## Investment Thesis Summary

Seanergy is a small-cap ($289M market cap), no-moat, Capesize/Newcastlemax dry bulk shipping company trading at a significant discount to peers and to estimated fleet NAV (~0.5x), with a credible three-year operational turnaround but real, structural governance concerns (super-voting preferred stock held by the CEO, who also leads a related company with recurring transactions).

**Bull thesis:** Cheapest multiples among direct dry bulk peers (EV/EBITDA 6.24x, forward P/E 5.57x); ~0.5x P/NAV discount offers meaningful re-rating potential even without a freight-rate boom; a $460 million newbuilding program (5 scrubber-fitted vessels) addresses the aging-fleet disadvantage over 2027-2029; management has shown capital discipline (cutting the dividend when earnings softened rather than over-committing); no vessel impairments through three years of financial statements including a declining-rate year.

**Bear thesis:** No structural moat — pure commodity/cyclical exposure via near-total BCI-index-linked revenue; average fleet age rising each year and management itself acknowledges competitors have newer, more attractive tonnage; super-voting preferred stock gives the CEO entrenched control acquired for nominal consideration; recurring related-party transactions with United Maritime Corporation (same CEO); a disclosed working capital deficit; TCE rates and dividend both fell sharply in FY2025 (-16% and -57% respectively).

## Decision Framework

**To buy at ~$13.34:**
- Confirmation that TCE rates are stabilizing near or above the Q1 2026 level ($24,219/day)
- Evidence of newbuilding program progressing on schedule/budget
- Any governance improvement narrowing the super-voting structure or increasing independent oversight of related-party dealings

**To avoid:**
- Continued TCE rate decline toward the daily opex breakeven level
- Evidence that related-party transactions with United Maritime are not being conducted on genuinely arm's-length terms
- Recurrence of proxy contests or other governance disruption
- Widening working capital deficit without a credible resolution plan

**Current stance: Speculative (High-Risk)**

Seanergy offers genuine statistical cheapness — the lowest multiples among direct dry bulk peers and a meaningful discount to estimated fleet NAV — but this is a name where the discount is plausibly, at least in part, a rational market response to real governance risk, not simply an overlooked opportunity. The combination of no structural moat, near-total commodity/cyclical exposure, an aging fleet, and a super-voting/related-party governance structure means this is appropriate only as a small, high-conviction-required position for investors comfortable with shipping-cycle risk AND willing to accept meaningfully weaker minority-shareholder protections than a typical U.S. domestic issuer would offer. This is not a "cheap and safe" value play — it is a leveraged bet on both the Capesize freight cycle and on management's continued good-faith stewardship despite structurally entrenched control.

**Key triggers for position changes:**
- Q2/Q3 2026 results confirming whether the Q1 2026 TCE recovery is durable or a temporary bounce
- Any disclosed governance reform (reduction in super-voting structure, enhanced related-party oversight)
- Resolution (positive or negative) of the working capital deficit via the post-year-end sale-and-leaseback arrangements referenced in the FY2025 20-F
- Newbuilding delivery milestones (first vessel expected 2027)
